# Log

Week 10

This week, the first materials needed for the initial assembly and tests arrived, including resistors, capacitors, soldering pins, the small 250 mAh battery, neodymium magnets, prototype boards, copper wire and batteries. Since the available prototype board was larger than the space planned inside the PocketQube, we had to manually cut it so that it could fit inside the 5 cm structure.

We also started the first tests related to the magnetorquers. Initially, we wrapped some copper wire around a nail and connected the ends to a battery, but no relevant effect was observed. Afterwards, we tested a circular copper coil and, when bfinging a magnet close to it, we were able to observe clear rotation. This test helped us better understand the operating principle of magnetorquers. At this stage, the first theoretical version of the power budget was also completed, based on datasheet values, although the power consumption of the coils was not yet included.
