# Log

This week, we progressed with the graphical visualization of sensor data. We continued testing the magnetorquers and realized that the initial coils needed more windings to produce a more visible effect. So, we achieved a first functional version of the magnetorquer system. We connected two coils to the circuit and, using the H-bridge, were able to control the direction of the current, observing the cube rotate when the current direction was inverted.

We also tested the power circuit using the larger battery and verified that all components worked correctly when powered by it. Later, we realized that the larger battery already included protection against excessive discharge, which allowed us to reconsider the use of the CN3065, as it is more suitable for managing charging through the solar panel. The final 3D model of the cube was also almost completed, already adapted to the new size and component layout.


We also attempted to integrate a current sensor to measure the current supplied by the solar panels and use that data in a future graphical interface. However, we found that the sensor we purchased could only measure currents above around 180 mA, while our solar panel provides a maximum current of approximately 11 mA, making it unsuitable for this application.
