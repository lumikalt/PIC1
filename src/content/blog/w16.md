Final magnetorquer and H-Bridge tests:
The cube was able to rotate approximately 90 degrees. However, the rotation was only possible towards three faces: front, left, and right, considering the camera face as the front reference. This limitation occurred because one of the H-Bridge pins was not working properly, as it was not supplying current.
