# Log

This week, the remaining materials arrived, including the new H-bridge, the charger module and some materials provided by IT, such as the support for hanging the cube and thinner copper wire. We then started testing the components connected to the computer and verified the joint operation of the ESP32 mini, the IMU and the magnetometer. The ESP32-CAM was also tested separately.

> Camera testing

We also tested the ESP32 mini, the IMU and the magnetometer powered by the larger battery, confirming that the system could operate correctly with this power source.
