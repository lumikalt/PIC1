# Log

This week, the remaining materials arrived, including the new H-bridge, the charger module and some materials provided by IT, such as the support for hanging the cube and thinner copper wire. We also started obtaining the first graphical visualization of the sensor data.

We continued testing the magnetorquers and realized that the initial coils needed more windings to produce a more visible effect. We then used thinner copper wire to wind a square coil, which allowed us to observe the suspended cube reacting when a magnet was brought close to it. We also tested the ESP32 mini, the IMU and the magnetometer powered by the larger battery, confirming that the system could operate correctly with this power source.

# Meetings

In another meeting with the IT professors:
- Discussed the coil tests and concluded that at least two coils placed on perpendicular faces would be needed to allow greater control over the cube’s orientation.
- Discussed the possibility of replacing permanent magnets with an external coil to create a more uniform magnetic field.
- At this stage, we decided not to manufacture a custom PCB designed in KiCad and instead use a prototype board. Nevertheless, the KiCad design work was useful to better understand the required connections and additional components, such as capacitors and resistors.
