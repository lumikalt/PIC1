# Log

During this week, new components arrived, namely the IMU, the magnetometer and the CN3065 solar charger. We also produced the first 3D print of the PocketQube, which allowed us to better evaluate the internal spacing and the quality of the structure.

<img src="/progress/pcb_diagram.jpg" alt="PCB diagram">
<img src="/progress/model_3dprint.jpeg" alt="Printed 3D model">

> Model for the sensors PCB and 3D printed satellite frame

Since the ESP32 mini had not arrived yet, we tested the magnetometer using an Arduino Uno as the microcontroller. Initially, the sensor was not working, but we realized that the pins needed to be soldered to the module. After soldering and making the correct connections, we were able to obtain magnetic field readings when approaching the sensor to a motor/magnet. At the same time, we began drawing the first connections in KiCad in order to understand how the components could be organized in a future PCB.
