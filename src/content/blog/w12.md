# Log

This week, the main missing components arrived, including the ESP32 mini, the ESP32-CAM and the H-bridge. We then started testing the components connected to the computer and verified the joint operation of the ESP32 mini, the IMU and the magnetometer. The ESP32-CAM was also tested separately.

<img src="/progress/w12/battery.jpeg" alt="ESP32-CAM testing" width=400>

> Camera testing

During the power system tests, we realized that the CN3065 solar charger did not include protection against excessive battery discharge. Since the ESP32 can continue consuming energy even when the system appears to be off, this could damage the battery. For this reason, we decided to order a TP4056 module with protection. We also concluded that the initial 250 mAh battery would not be enough to power the system in the worst-case scenario, so we bought a larger 3.7 V, 1200 mAh battery. In addition, it was necessary to order an extension cable for the ESP32-CAM and a new H-bridge in module format, since the first one ordered was only the chip.

<img src="/progress/battery.jpeg" alt="Battery testing" width=400>

> Battery testing, blue LED shows voltage regulation works.

After analyzing the available space in the first 3D model, we concluded that a 5 cm cube would be too small to accommodate all components, especially two boards, the coils and the new battery. Therefore, we decided to increase the cube size to 6 cm per side.
