# Log

So, we achieved a first functional version of the magnetorquer system. We connected two coils to the circuit and, using the H-bridge, were able to control the direction of the current, observing the cube rotate when the current direction was inverted.

The final 3D model of the cube was also almost completed, already adapted to the new size and component layout.

Finally, we tested the entire circuit, including the H-Bridges at 90º, which worked fully.
