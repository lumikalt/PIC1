## Mission defined

Simulate a PocketQube satellite in orbit mapping a city, as close as possible to how it's really done.

Real satellites of this kind have magnetorques to rotate around the altitude axis. To simulate this system, we can use a pair of Neodymium magnets, to simulate the magnetic field of the Earth, and inductor coils. This then allows us to control the satellite's rotation with the ESP32.

With the rotation being possible, we then move to executing the mission. With a model of a city around the satellite, we use its camera to take photos as it rotates. Then, the ESP32 sends the photos to the Ground Station via the antenna.
