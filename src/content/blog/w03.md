## Mission defined

Simulate a PocketQube satellite in orbit mapping a city.

Real satellites of this kind have magnetorques to rotate around the altitude axis. To simulate this system, we can use a pair of magnets, to simulate the magnetic field of the Earth, and inductor coils. This then allows us to control the satellite's rotation with the ESP32.

With the rotation being possible, we then move to executing the mission. With a model of a city around the satellite, we use its camera to take photos as it rotates. Then, the ESP32 produces a map, which is sent to the Ground Station via antenna.
